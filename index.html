Securing and investigating industrial systems — OT/ICS security & DFIR

GICSP · GIAC Advisory Board · Founder of DeskOps · Available June 2027

I'm Qin Xin, a final-year Computer Science and Design student at SUTD. My main focus is OT/ICS security: most of what I know comes from the GICSP and from building DeskOps, a hands-on OT security training platform for students. Alongside that, I'm building up digital forensics and incident response (DFIR), the other half of the job: working out what happened once something has gone wrong.

Currently

Right now I'm splitting my time between learning and building. At school I'm deep into compiler design, and on my own I'm working through network security and digital forensics, which is slowly turning packet captures and memory dumps from intimidating into fun. Most of my remaining time goes into DeskOps, where the team is gearing up for our first workshops: one at NYP in December, and a CSA-funded one in January.
